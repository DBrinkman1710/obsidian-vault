YIPPIE — COMMERCIAL WEBSITE UI REDESIGN PROMPT

── WHAT THIS IS ────────────────────────────────────────────────

Yippie is an AI-powered customer service platform for small and medium
businesses. The product handles inbox triage, tickets, live chat,
contacts, billing, and an activity feed — all in one place. The core
value: AI drafts your support tickets for you so you spend seconds
instead of minutes on every message.

The commercial website lives at getyippie.com (Next.js 14, TypeScript).
This prompt is for a full visual redesign of apps/web/src/app/page.tsx.

── THE BRAND ────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Brand blue : #5BA4F5 (Yippie's logo colour — warm cornflower blue)
White      : #FFFFFF (cards, nav, clean sections)
Slate-50   : #F8FAFC (alternating section background)
Text dark  : #0F172A (headings)
Text mid   : #475569 (body copy)
Text light : #94A3B8 (labels, captions)
Border     : #E2E8F0 (card borders, dividers)

The logo is the word "yippie" in white with a smiley-face mark to the
left (an arc, two dots). It always sits on the Yippie blue.

The product UI (the actual app) uses:
  - Yippie blue sidebar with white text and lucide-react SVG icons
  - White cards, rounded-2xl, shadow-sm, border border-slate-100
  - A 2×2 grid layout for the mail detail view (no emoji anywhere)
  - Inter / system-ui font, clean minimal spacing

── THE DESIGN DIRECTION ────────────────────────────────────────

IMPORTANT: This must NOT look like a generic AI-generated SaaS landing
page. Avoid every template cliché:

  ✗ No blue-to-purple gradient hero backgrounds
  ✗ No glassmorphism blobs or background glow circles
  ✗ No 3-column "icon + title + text" feature grid as the hero section
  ✗ No oversized "coming soon" countdown widgets
  ✗ No stock-photo-style mockup screenshots
  ✗ No particle effects, animated gradients, or parallax everything
  ✗ No "Trusted by 10,000+ teams" fake social proof banners
  ✗ No emoji used as icons anywhere — not in features, not in the nav

Look at these real sites for inspiration on what GOOD looks like:
  - linear.app — clean dark hero, crisp product screenshots, editorial
  - vercel.com — clear hierarchy, no noise, code speaks for itself
  - cal.com — simple, confident, shows the actual product UI
  - superhuman.com — single strong headline, premium feel, minimal

The Yippie site should feel:
  CONFIDENT — one strong headline, not three competing messages
  EDITORIAL — sections breathe, whitespace is intentional
  PRODUCT-FIRST — show the actual app, not a cartoon of it
  FAST — the page should feel as fast as the product claims to be

── VISUAL STYLE ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Typography:
  Font      : Inter (via next/font/google)
  H1        : text-5xl md:text-6xl lg:text-7xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
  H2        : text-3xl md:text-4xl, font-bold, tracking-tight
  Eyebrow   : text-xs font-semibold tracking-widest uppercase, text-yippie
  Body      : text-base md:text-lg, leading-relaxed, text-slate-600
  Caption   : text-sm, text-slate-400

Buttons:
  Primary   : bg-yippie text-white px-6 py-3 rounded-xl font-semibold
               hover:opacity-90 transition-opacity — NO gradient, no glow
  Ghost     : border border-slate-200 text-slate-700 px-6 py-3 rounded-xl
               hover:border-slate-400 transition-colors

Cards:
  Background: white
  Border    : border border-slate-100
  Radius    : rounded-2xl
  Shadow    : shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-shadow
  NO glassmorphism, no frosted blur on cards

Section rhythm:
  - Sections alternate white / slate-50 backgrounds
  - Max content width: max-w-6xl mx-auto
  - Padding: px-6 md:px-8, py-20 md:py-32 per section
  - Each section has ONE eyebrow, ONE headline, ONE sub-sentence

── SECTIONS ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. NAV
   Sticky. bg-white border-b border-slate-100.
   Logo (the SVG smiley mark + "yippie" wordmark) on the left.
   Three links: Features · Pricing · How it works
   Right: "Log in →" as a ghost button.
   No hamburger animation theatrics — simple, clean.

2. HERO
   White background. Two-column layout on desktop (text left, visual right).

   LEFT column:
     - Eyebrow tag: a small pill with Yippie blue dot — "AI customer service"
     - H1: "Give yourself back the time that matters"
     - Sub: 2 lines max. "Yippie auto-drafts every support ticket from
       your inbox. Review, approve, done."
     - Two buttons: "Start for free →" (primary) + "See how it works" (ghost)
     - No bullet points, no feature list in the hero

   RIGHT column:
     - An HTML/CSS mockup of the actual product interface:
       A browser chrome frame (rounded corners, three dots top-left)
       containing the Yippie app layout:
         - Left sidebar: Yippie blue (#5BA4F5), showing nav items as
           small rounded rectangles with one active state
         - Main area: 2×2 card grid (Customer | Email / Ticket | Reply)
           Each card: white, rounded-xl, with a thin header line and
           a few placeholder lines of content
     - Give the frame a drop-shadow-2xl and slight rotate-1 tilt
     - Behind it: a single soft Yippie-blue circle blur (opacity 15%,
       blur-3xl) — one, not five

3. LOGO BAR (social proof strip)
   Thin section. Gray text: "Teams at these businesses use Yippie"
   5 fictional SMB names in muted slate-400, no logos, no images.
   This section is minimal — it exists only to say "real businesses use this".

4. STATS
   Three stats in a row, centered, white background, generous spacing.
   Each stat: large number in #5BA4F5, small gray label below.
   "10h+" / "saved per week per person"
   "<2 min" / "average ticket response time"
   "6" / "modules, one platform"
   No borders between stats — use spacing alone to separate.

5. FEATURES
   Section heading: "Everything your support team needs"
   Layout: 2 columns on desktop, each row is a feature.
   Each feature row: lucide icon (20px, stroke 1.5) in a
   bg-slate-50 rounded-xl square LEFT, text RIGHT.
   Feature title bold, description gray. No card background — just
   the icon block and text on the section background.

   Features (icon from lucide-react):
     Inbox          / Smart inbox auto-drafts tickets from email & WhatsApp
     Ticket         / Track, assign, and close requests with SLA alerts
     MessageSquare  / Live chat widget — one line of code to embed
     Users          / Full customer history — emails, tickets, invoices
     Activity       / Real-time activity feed across your whole business
     CreditCard     / Send and track invoices without leaving the platform

6. HOW IT WORKS
   Slate-50 background.
   Three numbered steps in a horizontal row (desktop) or stacked (mobile).
   Large step number (01 / 02 / 03) in text-yippie/30, title, description.
   Between each step: a thin arrow line connecting them (desktop only).
   NO circular badges, no icons — the number IS the visual.

7. PRODUCT MOMENT (the "wow" section)
   Dark section: bg-slate-900. Full width.
   Headline (white): "Everything in one place"
   Sub (slate-400): one sentence about the unified platform.

   Below: a wide, centered HTML mockup of the inbox list page.
   Show the Yippie blue sidebar on the left (narrow, white nav items),
   and a list of 4–5 email rows on the right. Each row shows:
     sender name, subject, a small priority badge, a status pill.
   This is crisp, not decorative. It looks like the real product.

   NO neon glows, no floating elements, no confetti.

8. PRICING
   Section heading: "Simple, honest pricing"
   Three-column card layout. Middle card (Growth) has a Yippie blue
   border (border-2 border-yippie) and a small "Most popular" badge
   (bg-yippie text-white text-xs px-2 py-0.5 rounded-full).

   Show a monthly/annual toggle (annual = 20% off). Default monthly.

   Plans:
     Starter  €29/mo  1 user, 500 contacts, Inbox + Tickets, Live chat, Email support
     Growth   €79/mo  5 users, 5,000 contacts, All modules, Activity feed, Priority support
     Pro      €199/mo Unlimited users, Unlimited contacts, All modules, API access, Dedicated support

   Checkmark list uses a simple ✓ in text-yippie, not a circle badge.
   CTA button: primary for Growth, ghost for others.

9. CTA SECTION
   bg-yippie (solid brand blue — NOT a gradient).
   White headline: "Ready to win back your time?"
   White sub: "Join businesses that handle customer support in half the
   time. Start for free, no credit card required."
   One white button: "Start for free →" with bg-white text-yippie.

   This section should feel calm and confident, not loud.

10. FOOTER
    White. Logo left. Three links right: Features · Pricing · Log in.
    Bottom: © 2026 Yippie — muted gray, small.
    No newsletter signup, no social icons (keep it minimal).

── ANIMATIONS ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep animations purposeful and subtle:
  - Hero text fades in (opacity 0→1, translateY 8px→0, 400ms ease-out)
  - Feature rows animate in on scroll (Intersection Observer, stagger 80ms)
  - Card hover: shadow-md + subtle translateY(-2px)
  - Button hover: opacity-90 (no scale, no glow)
  - Pricing toggle: smooth transition between monthly/annual prices

Absolutely no:
  - Typewriter effects
  - Counter animations on stats (too gimmicky)
  - Floating animated elements
  - Scroll-jacked parallax
